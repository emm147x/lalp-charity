Desired outcomes & benefits

By 2030, we aim to change what's possible for vulnerable families.

We aim to improve maternal and child health outcomes and strengthen access to justice, welfare, and empowerment for vulnerable pregnant women and children through the LALP Initiative.

Budget overview

Where your ₦5,000,000 pilot contribution goes

To implement this initiative, we require ₦5 million to launch the pilot phase in Egbeda, covering essential infrastructure, empowerment programs, access-to-justice services, and operational costs.

Support for Vulnerable Pregnant Women 62.5%
Strengthened Access to Justice 25%
Enhanced Social and Welfare Services 12.5%

Metrics for success

How we track accountability

We track progress through monthly maternal and child health assessments, case-management reviews, and monitoring of justice and welfare support services — ensuring accountability and transparency throughout the LALP Initiative.
