About the organization

Justice, care, and dignity for people the system overlooks.

LALP Initiative was founded in 2020 to provide legal support and essential assistance to vulnerable individuals and those who cannot afford basic care.

Who we are

Founded to close the gap between rights on paper and rights in practice

The organization is dedicated to protecting the rights, wellbeing, and dignity of disadvantaged members of society — combining legal aid with the practical, everyday support that vulnerable families are so often denied.

Most of the people we work with are not without rights. They are without access: to a lawyer, to a clinic, to someone who will listen. LALP Initiative exists to close that gap.

Mission & vision

A world where dignity doesn't depend on income

Our vision is a world where every individual — especially children — has strengthened access to justice, education, healthcare, and the opportunities needed to live with dignity.

We pursue that vision through four connected programs: maternal health support, child welfare and protection, access to justice, and social and welfare empowerment — delivered together, because these hardships rarely arrive one at a time.
