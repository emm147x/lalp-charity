Issues addressed by the charity

The charity addresses hardship through four connected programs.

We deliver legal help, care services, and advocacy to ensure dignity, protection, and access to essential needs — for pregnant women, children, and disadvantaged families alike.

Group 01

Maternal Health Support

Focused assistance for vulnerable pregnant women through primary health-care services, regular check-ups, and essential supplies — delivered at the Primary Health Care ward level, where it's needed most.

Group 02

Child Welfare & Protection

Support systems for children, including health monitoring, nutrition assistance, and safe-community services — built around the child's whole environment, not just a single check-up.

Group 03

Access to Justice

Legal aid, rights awareness, and case-support services to empower women and ensure fair access to justice — from first consultation through to resolution.

Group 04

Social & Welfare Empowerment

Social safety-net programs, welfare assistance, and empowerment initiatives that strengthen resilience and improve long-term wellbeing — helping families stand on their own beyond the immediate crisis.

Every one of these programs runs on funding from people like you.
